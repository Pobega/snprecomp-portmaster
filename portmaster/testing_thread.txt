Game Information
Title: Sin and Punishment
URL: https://github.com/Pobega/snprecomp-portmaster

Instructions:
Copy your legally obtained Sin and Punishment Nintendo 64 ROM (.z64, .n64, or .v64) into ports/sinandpunishment/roms/ before launching. Optional translation patches (.bps, .ups, .ips, .xdelta) placed in roms/ are applied automatically on first boot. First launch takes about 5 seconds to verify the ROM and reconstruct the executable.

| Button | Action |
|--|--|
|DPAD| Strafe left / right (Aim crosshair while holding L1/L2 in Stickless; navigate menus) |
|Left Analog| Strafe left / right (Move character in Twin Stick) |
|Right Analog| Reticle aiming (Twin Stick) |
|A| Fire / Sword slash (Confirm in menus; Lock-on in Twin Stick) |
|B| Jump / Double jump (Cancel in menus) |
|X| Cycle lock-on targets / Quick crosshair re-center (Dodge roll in Twin Stick) |
|Y| Dodge roll |
|L1| Shift to manual aim (Stickless) / Jump (Twin Stick) / Strafe left (Bumper Strafe) |
|L2| Shift to manual aim (Stickless) / Jump (Twin Stick) / Dodge roll (Legacy) |
|R1| Fire / Sword slash |
|R2| Fire / Sword slash |
|Start| Pause game |
|Select| Open / Close Quick Settings overlay |
|Select + Start| Quick save and exit |
